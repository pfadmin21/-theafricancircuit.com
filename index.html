What is The African Circuit?

Where Institutions Shape Africa's Future

The African Circuit is a premier platform where investors, policymakers and business leaders connect to unlock opportunities, share insights and drive transformative investment across the continent.

  • High-level conferences and roundtables
  • Independent research and intelligence
  • Opportunity exchange and deal flow
  • Institutional network and membership

Mauritius Flagship

The Place Where Africa's Institutional Conversations Begin

Mauritius anchors the Circuit as the gateway between global capital and the African continent — a stable, treaty-connected jurisdiction long trusted by DFIs, fund managers and family offices structuring African exposure. Our flagship edition opens the calendar here for that reason: it is where the year's institutional conversation on Africa-bound capital begins, not where it concludes.

Over two days at the Caudan Arts Centre, 250+ senior decision-makers and 50+ global speakers convene across closed-door roundtables, panel dialogue and structured networking — covering blended finance, regulatory architecture, ESG and climate finance, and the deal flow shaping the continent's next decade.

14–15 September 2026
Caudan Arts Centre, Port Louis

Nairobi · East Africa Edition

The Circuit Continues Into East Africa's Capital Markets Hub

From Port Louis, the Circuit heads to Nairobi — venture and growth capital, sovereign debt, and the policy shifts drawing institutional capital deeper into the region. The East Africa Edition carries the same closed-door format into the market shaping the continent's next wave of deal flow.

Two days at the JW Marriott convene senior investors, DFIs and regional policymakers across panel dialogue and closed-door roundtables — building directly on the momentum set in Mauritius.

19–20 November 2026
JW Marriott, Nairobi
