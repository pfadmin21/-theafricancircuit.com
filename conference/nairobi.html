What This Edition Is Built to Deliver

East Africa's Capital Debate, Run in Parallel

Fintech to Credit Infrastructure

Where African fintechs go once payments alone stop being the business — the regulatory, data and lending infrastructure that comes next.

SME & Climate Capital

Alternative financing models for African SMEs and climate-adaptation technology, debated against the conventional bank-lending baseline.

A Live Dealroom

Day Two closes with a structured matchmaking session connecting fintech, SME and growth-capital opportunities directly with capital in the room.

The Nairobi Edition builds directly on the Circuit's Mauritius flagship. Full delegate and speaker confirmations go out directly to registered attendees closer to the date.

Debates & Theses

Three panels run as open debate. Two run as a thesis — a single provocative claim, argued out loud. Ten roundtables run underneath, closed door.

As with Mauritius, we publish the questions the room is built to fight over — not the roster answering them. Confirmations go out directly to registered delegates.
