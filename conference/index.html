What to Expect

A Working Conference, Not a Trade Show

The Mauritius Flagship is built around substance over spectacle — a small, senior room structured for real dialogue and deal flow, not a large audience watching from the back.

01

Main Stage Panels

Five moderated panels bringing DFIs, fund managers, regulators and rating agencies into direct dialogue on the capital and policy questions shaping African markets.

02

Closed-Door Roundtables

Eight small-group sessions held under Chatham House Rule — the space where the harder, more candid conversations on deal structuring and market access actually happen.

03

Structured Networking

Curated introductions and dedicated networking blocks between sessions, designed around who's in the room rather than left to chance.
