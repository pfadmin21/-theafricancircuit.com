What This Edition Is Built to Deliver

Convening Measured by Outcome, Not Attendance

Institutional Dialogue

Eight closed-door roundtables held under Chatham House Rule, designed to surface candid positions from DFIs, regulators and fund managers rather than panel-stage talking points.

Deal & Partnership Flow

Structured introductions between capital allocators and African-facing opportunities — the Circuit's Opportunity Exchange runs alongside the main programme to connect projects with the room.

A Standing Record

As the flagship edition, Mauritius sets the baseline the Circuit will report against — partnerships confirmed, dialogue held, and momentum carried into Nairobi and beyond.

The African Circuit is a first-edition convening. We report on what the programme is designed to achieve rather than project financial outcomes in advance — a full account of what the room actually produced will follow the event.

Open Debate

We don't publish a speaker list to fill seats. We publish the questions — because the debate is the point, and who's in the room to argue it is confirmed on invitation.

Every panel below is framed as the question it's actually built to fight over. Full delegate and speaker confirmations go out directly to registered attendees closer to the date.
