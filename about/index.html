The African Circuit

One circuit. Four cities.

The African Circuit brings together the institutions responsible for mandate, policy, structuring and deployment — on the continent, across the jurisdictions shaping Africa's next investment cycle. Mauritius · Nairobi · Cape Town · Casablanca.

Dialogue On the Continent

Every edition sits in an African jurisdiction, not a financial capital elsewhere. The people allocating and receiving capital are in the same room, on the same ground.

Trust That Compounds

Each closed-door roundtable and each edition builds on the last — the same institutions returning, the same conversations deepening rather than restarting from zero each time.

A Record, Not a Report

The Circuit isn't a research desk publishing conclusions about Africa from outside — it's the room itself, and what gets decided in it.

The Circuit

From mandate to deployment.

Capital exists. Mandates exist. Projects exist. The gap is deployment. The African Circuit brings together the institutions responsible for mandate, policy, structuring and deployment across the African continent.

Explore the editionsAttend Nairobi
Africa does not lack opportunity. It needs stronger circuits of trust.

Capital moves through relationships, governance, credible information and the right institutional vehicles. The African Circuit brings these elements together by convening the institutions that shape how capital is mandated, structured and deployed.

Each edition is designed to deepen the conversation rather than restart it — connecting investors, DFIs, family offices, fund managers, regulators, policymakers, corporate leaders, project sponsors and professional advisers.

Inside the Circuit

Four actions. One continuous conversation.

The Circuit is built around what needs to happen for institutional capital to move: the right people must meet, the barriers must be interrogated, relevant pathways must connect, and the conversation must continue between editions.

01

Convene

Bring decision-makers and institutions with a real role in capital formation into the same room.

02

Interrogate

Examine the practical policy, structuring, risk and execution barriers preventing capital deployment.

03

Connect

Bring mandates, policy, structures, institutions and investable opportunities into the same conversation.

04

Continue

Carry relationships, questions and institutional dialogue from one African financial centre to the next.
