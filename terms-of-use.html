02Who can use the Circuit

The Circuit is for professionals, including investors, development finance institutions, regulators, fund managers, family offices, advisers and project owners. By registering, you confirm that:

  • you are at least 18;
  • you are acting in a professional capacity;
  • if you register on behalf of an organisation or colleagues, you are authorised to do so.

03Registration

  • Sending the registration form is a request for a place. It is not yet a booking.
  • A booking is made when we confirm it by email. It is secured when the invoice is paid.
  • We may decline or cancel a registration at our discretion. Examples include when an edition is full or the participant profile does not fit the edition. If we cancel after you have paid, we refund what you paid.
  • Each access pass is personal to the named delegate and gives access only to the sessions included in that access category.
  • Closed-door sessions are curated by invitation and are not part of any access category.
  • You must give accurate information. We may refuse entry if the person attending is not the confirmed delegate.

04Prices & payment

  • Prices are in US dollars and follow a release schedule, rising as each release opens. The price shown on the Site when you submit your registration is the price that applies to you, as long as the invoice is paid by its due date.
  • Prices shown on the Site exclude taxes and fees. Any applicable taxes and fees are confirmed on your invoice before you pay.
  • Payment is made against our invoice, using the methods and within the time stated on it. We do not take card payments on the Site.
  • If an invoice is not paid on time, we may release the place. Any new booking is then at the prices in force at that time.
  • Prices do not include travel, accommodation, visas or insurance. These are your responsibility.
  • If a price on the Site is clearly wrong, we will tell you before invoicing and you may withdraw your registration.

05Substitutions & cancellations

Substitutions

If you cannot attend, a colleague from the same organisation may take your place at no extra cost. Let us know in writing with their details before the edition.

Cancellations by you

Cancellations must be sent to us in writing. Any refund depends on the cancellation terms stated in your confirmation or invoice.

Cancellation by us

If we cancel an edition, we will offer you either a full refund of the fees you paid or a transfer to another date or edition. If we postpone an edition, you may choose to keep your place or receive a full refund. We are not responsible for travel or accommodation costs you have incurred, so we recommend flexible bookings and travel insurance.

We may need to change the programme, speakers, timings, format or, in exceptional cases, the venue. We will tell registered delegates about significant changes as early as possible.

Changes to individual speakers or sessions do not entitle you to a refund. If we change the dates or city of an edition, the postponement and cancellation terms in section 05 apply.
